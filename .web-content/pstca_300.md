**_300 Bus Power Flow Test Case_**
The IEEE 300 bus test case was been developed by the IEEE Test Systems Task Force under the direction of Mike Adibi in 1993.

The [IEEE Common Data Format](https://labs.ece.uw.edu/pstca/pf300/pg_tca300bus.htm) version was obtained from Fernando Alvarado at the University of Wisconsin. The [PTI format](https://labs.ece.uw.edu/pstca/pf300/pg_tca300bus.htm) version was provided courtesy of Joan Staron of PTI.
